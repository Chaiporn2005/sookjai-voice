2 · Problem Statement

Healthcare is hard to reach for older adults

“ผู้สูงอายุเข้าถึงการดูแลสุขภาพยากผ่านการไปโรงพยาบาล,การเดินทางไปโรงพยายาล,โรค NCDs, และปัญหาด้านจิตใจ เช่น ความเหงา ความโดดเดี่ยว (Social Isolation) รวมถึงช่องว่างทางเทคโนโลยี”Participant's framing, verbatim [source: participant]

Current customer journey [source: participant]

  • Basic care means going to hospital, and the travel itself is a barrier
  • NCDs need ongoing attention that is hard to get without visits
  • Loneliness and social isolation
  • A technology gap makes digital tools hard to use
  • Fear of seeing a doctor (“ช่วยลดความกลัวในการพบแพทย์” — the aim of reducing that fear)

5 · Competitive Landscape — build vs. buy and gaps

Build the experience, partner for the rest

Build vs. buy

  • Partner or refer: telemedicine and doctor consults [source: web research]
  • Build (done): Thai voice companion, triage and safety rules [source: prototype]
  • Buy: language and speech models (Typhoon API) [source: prototype]
  • Research or license later: SpO2, Vision and ML vitals [source: web research]

Market gaps

  • No product found that combines a Thai voice companion, triage and a camera pulse check for older adults [source: assumption]
  • National apps focus on service access rather than daily companionship [source: assumption]

6 · Feasibility Assessment

Six dimensions

Technical Go

Working prototype; test suites pass (core 70, Worker 34, prosody 15, end-to-end 40); live Typhoon run succeeded [source: prototype]

Clinical validity Conditional

PPG not validated on people; camera PPG needs rigorous validation [source: web research — PMC scoping review]; voice-mood accuracy unvalidated [source: prototype]

Regulatory & privacy Conditional

Thai SaMD labeling/UDI rules apply to risk classes 2–4 from 20 Jun 2026 [source: web research — Tilleke & Gibbins]; SpO2 or diagnosis claims would likely trigger SaMD classification [source: assumption]; consent and on-device processing implemented [source: prototype]

Resource & capability Conditional

No clinician reviewer yet [source: prototype]; advisor roles unfilled [source: assumption]

Timeline Go (demo) Conditional (pilot)

Demo works today; pilot needs validation first [source: prototype]

Budget / cost Conditional

Re-rated from Go after A6 was rejected: free tiers (Typhoon speech recognition 100 req/min, chat 200 req/min at time of writing) are not enough for the pilot; paid pricing was not found [source: prototype README] [source: participant — A6 rejected]

Overall verdict: Conditional Go, confidence medium.

8 · Architecture / How It Works

On the device first, AI only through the Worker

flowchart LR
  subgraph PHONE["Browser app (one HTML page)"]
    P1["Camera PPG · two BPM estimates + quality gate"]
    P2["Symptom rules · offline chat"]
    P3["Safety layer · 1669 / 1323 answered locally"]
    P4["Voice-mood · pitch, loudness, rate, pauses vs. usual voice"]
    P5["Microphone → 16 kHz WAV"]
    P6["Thai speech of the reply"]
  end
  W["Cloudflare Worker · Typhoon key + locked safety prompt"]
  T1["Typhoon speech recognition"]
  T2["Typhoon language model"]
  P5 --> W --> T1
  W --> T2
  T2 --> W --> P6
  P4 -. "cue words only" .-> W
  • Pulse measurement, symptom rules, offline chat and voice-mood analysis all run on the device; only text size, consent, and the user's usual-voice values (only when voice-mood is on) are kept in the browser [source: prototype]
  • Camera PPG: fingertip video, then filtering, then two independent BPM estimates and a quality gate (score ≥ 65, agreement within 15%) [source: prototype]
  • Voice path: the microphone is recorded in the browser as a 16 kHz WAV, sent to the Cloudflare Worker, which holds the Typhoon key and a locked safety prompt; Typhoon speech recognition produces text and the Typhoon language model produces the reply; the browser speaks it in Thai [source: prototype]
  • Safety layer: emergency and self-harm phrases are matched on the device first and answered locally (1669 / 1323) without reaching the AI [source: prototype]
  • Voice-mood: only cue words such as “พูดเร็วกว่าปกติ” are sent; the AI weighs the user's words above voice cues; the user confirms with yes or no [source: prototype]
  • Hosting: GitHub Pages for the page; Cloudflare Workers for the proxy [source: prototype]

13 · Key Risks & Mitigations

What could go wrong

#RiskMitigation
R1PPG inaccurate in older adults, especially with irregular rhythms (UCSF: 0.9 agreement with ECG; all disagreements in irregular rhythms) [source: web research — MobiHealthNews]Quality gate (score ≥ 65, agreement within 15%); validation study (SC1)
R2AI gives wrong or unsafe advice [source: prototype]Locked system prompt, local safety rules, disclaimers, clinician review (S4)
R3Voice reply latency of about 15 s in one live run [source: prototype]Faster model, shorter replies, progress cues; target SC3
R4Voice-mood false alarms cause worry [source: prototype]Opt-in, user confirmation, words weighted above voice, DMH ST-5/2Q
R5Regulatory exposure if SpO2 or diagnosis claims are made [source: web research — Tilleke & Gibbins]Keep SpO2 and diagnosis on the roadmap until validated
R6Adoption blocked by the digital gap [source: participant]Voice-first, large text; family and อสม. onboarding
R7API cost beyond free tiers; pilot cannot run on free quota (A6 rejected) [source: participant]Price the paid tier, estimate cost per user, fund via partners or public programs (A5)
I1Updated Worker code not yet redeployed [source: prototype]Redeploy in the Cloudflare dashboard

16 · Go / No-Go

Verdict: Conditional Go

Technically ready for the demo and a pilot. Health claims beyond “first-line guidance” must wait for validation and a clear regulatory position.

Conditions

  • C1 PPG meets SC1 (MAE ≤ 5 BPM on ≥ 30 older adults) before any accuracy claim
  • C2 Clinician review of the symptom rules and AI prompt before the pilot
  • C3 A funded API plan is in place before the pilot (A6 rejected)
  • C4 No SpO2 or diagnosis claims until validated and the regulatory position is clear

Next steps (Inception)

  • PPG validation protocol
  • Recruit advisors and run the review
  • Pilot budget plan
  • Latency work toward ≤ 6 s
  • Identify a pilot partner (รพ.สต. / อสม.)
